## 0.1.3 — 2026-10-22

### Changed
- Comandante General is Simón Bolívar's own mechanic: no longer in the unique unit menu; Bolívar always gets it, other players never.
- Dido can move her capital to any city with a harbor (Harbor, Cothon or Royal Navy Dockyard); other players with a Cothon no longer see this project.

## 0.1.2 — 2026-10-18

### Fixed
- Ability bonuses for districts and buildings no longer stack 2–3 times (Brazil campus +9, Sundiata's Sukiennice 4 writing slots).
- Players who got the mod while joining a lobby now get a draft: the host rolls their items, their civilization is their leader's.
- Better Balanced Starts no longer fails with a civilization nobody picked in the menu (e.g. Mongolia).

### Added
- Host chat note for players without the draft menus: rejoin the lobby.
- Stock binding state and host rolls in the host's Lua.log.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-10-18

- First public version: lobby draft of leader, civilization and four unique items for Gathering Storm multiplayer, secret Random picks, Stock binding option, BBS start biases, up to 12 players.
